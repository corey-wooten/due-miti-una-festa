Travel Notes
Flights

Book outbound flights as early as possible - target spring 2027. The return from Palermo has fewer direct options so lock that in early too. All attendees should plan to arrive in Venice by 3:00 PM on July 21.

Italy Rail

Download the Trenitalia app. All trains listed use 1st class where specified - book 30–60 days ahead. Never buy at the station. Always validate regional train tickets before boarding or face a fine.

Tours

All three guided tours (Tuscany, Rome by Night, Pompeii/Vesuvius) are bookable via GetYourGuide. Links are included in the itinerary. Prices are per current listings and subject to change - book well in advance for summer availability.

Overnight Ferry

The Grandi Navi Veloci Naples–Palermo crossing takes approximately 11 hours overnight. Private cabins will be booked in advance for the group. Dinner and breakfast service are available on board - a far more comfortable option than a late-night flight.

Money & SIM Card

Italy uses the Euro. A Wise or Schwab debit card covers foreign fees and ATM charges. Pick up a prepaid SIM at any Italian airport newsstand on arrival (~€15/10GB). Carry some cash - smaller vendors and markets often prefer it.

Group Coordination

All addresses, confirmation numbers, and schedules will be shared via the WhatsApp group before departure. For scheduled departures - trains, ferries, tours - be on time. Anyone who misses a departure is responsible for catching up independently.
